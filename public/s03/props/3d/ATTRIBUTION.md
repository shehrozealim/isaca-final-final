# External 3D asset provenance

Downloaded for this site's desk scene on 2026-10-09. Files are served locally; no runtime external API calls.

- Television 01, Gabriel Radić / Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/Television_01 . CC0. Original glTF geometry and 2K diffuse, normal, and AO/roughness/metal maps.
- ScatteringSkull, Vladimir Petkovic / Khronos glTF Sample Assets: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ScatteringSkull . CC0. Unsupported scattering material extensions removed; geometry retained and lit with an aged bone material.
- Metal Plate / Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate . CC0. 1K color, normal and roughness maps.
- Brown Leather / Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather . CC0. 1K color, normal and roughness maps.

Sword and candlestick geometry, wick, wax drips, flame shader, screen and choreography are original code. Supplied reference and existing illustration assets remain in the project.
